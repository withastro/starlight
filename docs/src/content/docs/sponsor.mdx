Starlight is part of the [Astro](https://astro.build) project.
Sponsorships are collected through [Astro's Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/astrodotbuild) and support work across the Astro ecosystem, including Starlight.

## Community maintained

Starlight's releases, maintenance, and continued development are made possible by its community of maintainers and contributors.
Their day-to-day work sustains a high-quality, low-footprint solution for developer documentation.
With additional integrations, Starlight can power your entire web presence, including marketing pages and a blog!

If you rely on Starlight, sponsorship is a great way to support the project and help us continue to build and maintain it.

## Sponsorship

One-time contributions help fund current work and are welcome at any amount.
Recurring sponsorships have an even greater impact because predictable funding gives maintainers the time to:

- maintain Starlight while preserving the quality and stability users rely on
- improve accessibility, performance, and documentation
- develop features and improvements that require sustained work
- provide plugin authors better APIs and tooling

We also recognize some levels of sponsorship with a logo and/or link on the [Starlight website](/) and [GitHub README](https://github.com/withastro/starlight/blob/main/packages/starlight/README.md).
We are always happy to [work directly](mailto:partner@astro.build) with larger sponsors ($2000+ per month) to create a custom sponsorship package.

## Fund development

If your project needs a feature Starlight does not provide, or you lack the time or expertise to build a plugin, you can partner with us to fund one for your product or service.

Plugins can customize Starlight's configuration, UI, and behavior while also being easy to share and reuse.
[A plugin](/resources/plugins/) can make your product or service easier to use with Starlight and benefit the wider ecosystem.

Funded work must align with Starlight's goals and follow the project contribution process.
