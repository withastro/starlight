Trusted by OpenAI, GitHub, Netlify, Microsoft, Sentry, and [more](https://github.com/withastro/starlight/network/dependents), Starlight is an entirely community-maintained and full-featured theme for lightweight, performant, accessible, and eco-friendly documentation.
A healthy ecosystem of [plugins](/resources/plugins/) and [themes](/resources/themes/) lets you extend and customize it to fit your needs.

Starlight is part of the [Astro](https://astro.build) project.
Sponsorships are collected through [Astro's Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/astrodotbuild) and support work across the Astro ecosystem, including Starlight.

## Community maintained

Starlight's releases, maintenance, and continued development are made possible by its community of maintainers and contributors.
Their day-to-day work sustains a high-quality, low-footprint solution for developer documentation.
Beyond documentation, community plugins and Astro integrations let Starlight power your entire web presence, including marketing pages and a blog!

If you rely on Starlight, sponsorship is a great way to support the project and help its maintainers continue to build and maintain it.

## Sponsorship

One-time contributions help fund current work and are welcome at any amount.
Recurring sponsorships have an even greater impact because predictable funding gives maintainers the time to:

- maintain Starlight and preserve the quality and stability users rely on
- improve accessibility, performance, and documentation
- develop features and improvements that require sustained work
- provide plugin authors better APIs and tooling

We also recognize some levels of sponsorship with a logo and/or link on the [Starlight website](/) and [GitHub README](https://github.com/withastro/starlight/blob/main/packages/starlight/README.md).
We are always happy to [work directly](mailto:partner@astro.build) with larger sponsors ($2,000+ per month) to create a custom sponsorship package.

## Fund development

If your project needs a feature Starlight does not provide, or you lack the time or expertise to build a plugin yourself, you can partner with us to fund its development.

Plugins can customize Starlight's configuration, UI, and behavior while also being easy to share and reuse.
[A plugin](/resources/plugins/) can make your product or service easier to use with Starlight and benefit the wider ecosystem.

Funded work is carried out by Astro and Starlight maintainers, who build the APIs your feature or plugin will rely on.
It must align with Starlight's goals and follow the project [contribution process](https://github.com/withastro/starlight/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
